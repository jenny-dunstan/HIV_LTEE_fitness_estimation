Data required for the analysis files. Sequence data is too large to upload here, but is available on Zenodo at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24590954 

Deep Mutational Scanning (DMS) data is from the following two papers:

Haddox, Hugh K., Adam S. Dingens, Sarah K. Hilton, Julie Overbaugh, and Jesse D. Bloom. 2018. ‘Mapping Mutational Effects along the Evolutionary Landscape of HIV Envelope’. eLife 7 (March): e34420. https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.34420.

Hong, Zhenchen, Kai S. Shimagaki, and John P. Barton. 2024. ‘popDMS Infers Mutation Effects from Deep Mutational Scanning Data’. Bioinformatics 40 (8): btae499. https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btae499.
