Data required for the analysis files. Sequencing data is too large to upload here, but is available on Zenodo at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24590954 

Sequencing data, and all other data from the long term evolution experiment can also be found at:

Bons, Eva, Christine Leemann, Karin J. Metzner, and Roland R. Regoes. 2020. ‘Long-Term Experimental Evolution of HIV-1 Reveals Effects of Environment and Mutational History’. PLOS Biology 18 (12): e3001010. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.3001010.

Movasati, Ali, Christine Leemann, Kathrin Neumann, et al. 2026. ‘Substantial Deceleration of Adaptation of HIV-1 within 1500 Generations in an Experimental Evolution: A Genomic Perspective’. Virus Evolution 12 (1): veag059. https://doi.org/10.1093/ve/veag059.


Deep Mutational Scanning (DMS) data is from the following two papers:

Haddox, Hugh K., Adam S. Dingens, Sarah K. Hilton, Julie Overbaugh, and Jesse D. Bloom. 2018. ‘Mapping Mutational Effects along the Evolutionary Landscape of HIV Envelope’. eLife 7 (March): e34420. https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.34420.

Hong, Zhenchen, Kai S. Shimagaki, and John P. Barton. 2024. ‘popDMS Infers Mutation Effects from Deep Mutational Scanning Data’. Bioinformatics 40 (8): btae499. https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btae499.
